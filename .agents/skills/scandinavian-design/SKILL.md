# Scandinavian Design Engineering

A refined, calm, and hyper-legible design philosophy rooted in Nordic design and Dieter Rams principles:

## 1. Core Principles
- **Monochrome & Neutral Foundation**: Clean dark obsidian, pure crisp white, slate grays. Never use rainbow neon gradients.
- **Generous Spacing (Space > Lines)**: Use generous whitespace (gap: 24px to 48px) rather than enclosing every single item inside heavy boxes and borders.
- **Restrained Typography**: High typographic hierarchy. Large, confident, thin or semibold metric displays paired with muted uppercase metadata labels.
- **Micro Contrast**: Let the background breathe through transparent glass surfaces. Content floats effortlessly without visual heaviness.
- **Purposeful Accents**: Only one primary accent color at a time (e.g. Precision Emerald for nominal status, Amber for warning, Rose for critical).
